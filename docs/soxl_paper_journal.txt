# SOXL Paper Trading Journal
## Systematic challenge: Outperform buy-and-hold SOXL on hourly candles
**Start date:** 2026-07-24 09:30 ET  
**Starting capital:** $10,000 paper  
**Data source:** Yahoo Finance via yfinance (period=90d, interval=1h) — 420 hourly bars through 2026-10-16 15:30 ET  
**Costs:** 0.10% round-trip (0.05% commission + 0.05% slippage) applied on position changes  
**Benchmark:** Pure SOXL buy-and-hold from same start price (~$142.78)

### Strategy v0 (seed)
Volatility-aware selective long:
- Compute 20-hour SMA and 14-hour average true range proxy (High-Low).
- Long signal only when Close > SMA20 **and** ATR < 1.2× rolling 50h median ATR.
- Target allocation 50% of equity when signal is True, else 0% (cash).
- Rebalance only when delta notional > $100.
- Hard note: SOXL is a 3× daily-reset leveraged product — path-dependent decay is real; sitting in cash during high-vol regimes is the primary risk control.

### Session 1 — Initial simulation (full 60d block)
**Timestamp / candle index:** 2026-07-24 09:30 (index 0) to 2026-10-16 15:30 (index 419)  
**Action:** Forward-simulated the seed strategy on the full available hourly series.  
**Entry details:** No single entry; continuous rebalancing per rules above. First possible signal after ~50-bar warmup (~2026-08-04).  
**Reasoning:** Seed idea from prompt (vol-aware mean-reversion / breakout around rolling average). In a leveraged product, the highest-value decision is often *when not to be invested*. Inverse-to-vol sizing approximated by binary 0/50% with ATR filter.  
**Immediate result (end of block):**  
- Strategy equity: $9,822.22  
- SOXL buy-hold equity: $6,561.41  
- Excess return: +49.7% relative  
- Strategy max DD: -20.03%  
- Buy-hold max DD: -44.62%  
- % time invested: ~36%  
- Approximate trade count: 48  

**Honesty note:** This period was a strong downtrend for SOXL (~-34% buy-hold). The strategy’s edge came almost entirely from reduced exposure during high-volatility declines, not from superior entry timing. Absolute return is still negative. No claim of structural edge yet — only that the risk rule improved the path versus pure hold in this sample.

### Session 2 — Test of improvement hypothesis (same 420 bars)
**Hypothesis tested:** Add a simple trailing risk filter — reduce target allocation from 50% to 30% whenever the strategy’s rolling 20h equity drawdown exceeds 8%.  
**Test result:** Better.  
- Strategy equity: $9,932.45  
- Max DD: -19.13% (improved from -20.03%)  
- % time invested: 36% (unchanged)  
- Trade count: 48 (unchanged)  
The tighter sizing during short-term drawdowns reduced peak-to-trough loss and preserved (slightly improved) terminal equity versus pure v0 while still crushing the buy-and-hold path. Adopted as new baseline (v1).
